OUR STORY

A Place of Shade, A Place of Welcome

Kivuli — Swahili for "shade" — is exactly the haven we built for you: a cool, quiet retreat in the heart of Kanduyi, Bungoma, designed for travellers, families, and locals alike.

From our acclaimed restaurant serving Kenyan and continental favourites — including our famous nyama choma — to our comfortable guest rooms, sparkling swimming pool, lively bar, and sauna, every corner of Kivuli is crafted for relaxation, flavour, and memory-making.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including all public holidays. Our kitchen never closes, so you can enjoy a meal at any time, day or night.

Do you accept walk-in guests, or should I book?

Both! Walk-ins are always welcome at our restaurant, bar and swimming pool. However, we recommend booking ahead for accommodation and private events to guarantee availability. You can book instantly through the form on this website or call us directly.

Do you offer delivery or takeaway?

Yes! We offer both takeaway and delivery within Bungoma town and Kanduyi. Call +254 110 096 089 to place your order.

Can I host a wedding, birthday, or company event at Kivuli?

Absolutely. We regularly host weddings, engagement parties, birthdays, graduation celebrations, church functions, and corporate events/conferences. Our garden and indoor spaces can cater for groups of various sizes. Send us an enquiry with your date and expected number of guests, and we'll tailor a package for you.

Is parking available?

Yes — we offer free, secure private parking within our compound, 24/7.

Do you have Wi-Fi and M-Pesa payment?

Yes to both. Free Wi-Fi is available for guests, and we accept M-Pesa, cash, and card payments.

Can I use the swimming pool without staying overnight?

Yes — day visitors are welcome to use the swimming pool for a small daily fee. Food and drinks can be ordered poolside.
